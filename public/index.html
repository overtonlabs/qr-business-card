QR Business Card Generator

Generate a QR code that holds your contact details outright. Scanning it opens the native contact viewer on the phone doing the scanning, so the details can be saved in one tap. Print it on a business card, a flyer, or the back of your phone.

The code contains a vCard directly. It does not link to a website, a tracking redirect, or any third-party service, so it keeps working with no internet connection and no app installed. This page loads nothing from anywhere else either — the contact details you type never leave your browser.

Contact information
